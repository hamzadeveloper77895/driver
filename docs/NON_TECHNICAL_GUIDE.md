# ChauffeurX Plain-English Guide (NON_TECHNICAL_GUIDE.md)

This guide explains how **ChauffeurX** works in simple, everyday language for non-technical stakeholders, business owners, and new users.

---

## 1. What is ChauffeurX? (The Big Idea)
Think of ChauffeurX like Uber or Ola, but with one major difference:
- In regular ride-sharing, the driver arrives in **their** car and drives you.
- In **ChauffeurX**, you have **your own car** (e.g. you don't want to drive in heavy traffic, you are going to a party, or you want a road trip), and you hire a **professional chauffeur** who arrives at your doorstep to drive **your personal car**.

---

## 2. How the Customer Uses the App (Customer Journey)

### Step 1: 10-Second Sign Up
1. The customer opens the app and enters their 10-digit mobile phone number.
2. They get a 6-digit text message code (OTP) and type it in. No complicated passwords needed.
3. They enter their name and email (for ride receipts).

### Step 2: Add Their Car to "My Garage"
Before booking, the customer tells the app what car they own:
- Car name (e.g., *Honda City* or *Hyundai Creta*).
- Plate number (e.g., *KA 01 AB 1234*).
- **Automatic or Manual transmission**: This is the most important part! If you drive an automatic car, the app makes sure to only send you a driver who knows how to drive an automatic car.

### Step 3: Choose Where and How Long
1. The customer taps "Where to?" and types an address or picks a spot on the map.
2. They select a time package: **2 Hours**, **4 Hours**, or **Full Day**.
3. The app shows the exact estimated price upfront (e.g. $29.50) with no hidden fees.

### Step 4: Finding a Driver & The Secret 4-Digit Code
1. The customer taps **"Book Driver"**.
2. The system searches for the closest available driver nearby.
3. Once accepted, the customer sees the driver's name, photo, phone number, and a **Secret 4-Digit PIN** (like `8492`).
4. **Safety Rule**: The driver cannot start driving or charge money until they arrive and the customer gives them this 4-digit PIN in person.

### Step 5: The Ride, Safety & Payment
1. The driver arrives, the customer shares the PIN, and the trip starts.
2. The customer can track the car live on the map, chat with the driver, or hit an **SOS Button** if there is ever an emergency.
3. When the trip ends, the fare is automatically paid from the customer's in-app wallet or paid in cash, and the customer can rate the driver 1 to 5 stars.

## 3. How the Driver Uses the App (Driver Journey)

### Step 1: Driver Registration & Going Online
1. The driver signs in with their mobile number.
2. When the driver is ready to work, they flip a big switch on their screen from **OFFLINE** to **GO ONLINE**.
3. Once online, the app tracks their location to see which customers are close by.

### Step 2: Receiving a Ride Request
1. When a nearby customer books, the driver's phone chimes with a **30-second alert**.
2. The screen shows:
   - How far away the customer is.
   - What car they will drive (e.g., *Automatic Transmission Honda City*).
   - How many hours the booking is for and how much money the driver will earn.
3. The driver taps **"Accept Trip"**.

### Step 3: Arriving & Starting the Trip
1. The driver follows built-in map navigation to reach the customer's home or office.
2. When they pull up, they tap **"I Have Arrived"** (the customer gets a notification).
3. The driver greets the customer and asks: *"Could you please share your 4-digit start code?"*
4. The driver types that 4-digit code into their phone. This officially starts the trip clock.

### Step 4: Finishing the Trip & Getting Paid
1. The driver drives the customer wherever they need to go.
2. At the destination, the driver taps **"End Trip"**.
3. If the customer booked via Wallet, the screen says: *"Payment settled automatically. Do not collect cash."* If cash, the screen tells the driver the exact cash amount to collect.
4. The driver can check their **Earnings Screen** anytime to see their daily income and weekly bank transfers.

## 4. How the Admin Panel Works (Platform Management)

The Admin is the business owner or platform supervisor who runs the business through a control dashboard:

1. **Driver Approval**: Admins review driver licenses and background checks before letting them go online.
2. **Pricing Control**: Admins can change prices anytime (e.g. increase base price, adjust night fees from 10 PM to 6 AM, or launch discount promo codes).
3. **Live Monitoring**: Admins can see all active rides happening across the city in real time on a master map.
4. **Disputes & Safety**: If an SOS is triggered or a customer complains about rude behavior, the admin can inspect the ride history, review GPS logs, and refund wallets or suspend accounts.
